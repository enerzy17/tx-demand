1. The statute fixes the due date

Tex. Prop. Code § 28.002(b) is not a guideline. A contractor who receives an owner's payment "shall pay each of its subcontractors the portion of the owner's payment ... not later than the seventh day after the date the contractor receives the owner's payment."

“The payment required by this subsection must be made not later than the seventh day after the date the contractor receives the owner's payment.”
§ 28.002(b)

2. Interest runs at 1.5 percent a month

Tex. Prop. Code § 28.004(a) starts interest the day after the payment becomes due. § 28.004(b) sets the rate:

“An unpaid amount bears interest at the rate of 1-1/2 percent each month.”
§ 28.004(b), 18 percent a year

Interest accrued from 23 September 2026 to today is $409.51. It keeps accruing until the date of delivery, the date of mailing, or judgment (§ 28.004(c)).

3. Costs and attorney's fees follow the principal

Tex. Prop. Code § 28.005(b): in an action under the chapter the court “may award costs and reasonable attorney's fees as the court determines equitable and just.”

4. Work can be suspended without liability for delay

Tex. Prop. Code § 28.009 lets a subcontractor suspend performance on 10 days' written notice to the owner and the owner's lender stating that payment has not been received and that performance will be suspended for nonpayment. A subcontractor who suspends under the section is not liable for the delay.

The statute, verbatim

§ 28.002(b). “A contractor who receives a payment under Subsection (a) or otherwise from an owner in connection with a contract to improve real property shall pay each of its subcontractors the portion of the owner's payment, including interest, if any, that is attributable to work properly performed or materials suitably stored or specially fabricated as provided under the contract by that subcontractor, to the extent that subcontractor's interest in the owner's payment. The payment required by this subsection must be made not later than the seventh day after the date the contractor receives the owner's payment.”

§ 28.004. “(a) An unpaid amount required under this chapter begins to accrue interest on the day after the date on which the payment becomes due. (b) An unpaid amount bears interest at the rate of 1-1/2 percent each month.”

§ 28.005(b). “In an action brought under this chapter, the court may award costs and reasonable attorney's fees as the court determines equitable and just.”

§ 53.052(b). A claimant other than an original contractor must file the affidavit with the county clerk “not later than the 15th day of the fourth month after the later of: (1) the month the claimant last provided labor or materials; or (2) the month the claimant would normally have been required to deliver the last of specially fabricated materials.”
